About Atari Farms

Atari Farms Limited is a Ugandan climate-smart agribusiness focused on reducing post-harvest losses, strengthening market access for smallholder farmers, and transforming agricultural produce into high-value products through circular value addition.

Our Mission

Reducing post-harvest losses and strengthening market access for smallholder farmers across Eastern Uganda through climate-smart agribusiness solutions.

Our Approach

Working with 1500+ smallholder farmers through aggregation, processing, market linkage, innovation, and capacity building.

Circular Economy

Transforming agricultural by-products into high-value products, reducing waste, and creating sustainable income streams.

Sustainability

Promoting climate-smart practices, youth participation, women empowerment, and long-term environmental value creation.

The Challenge We Address

Limited Market Access

Smallholder farmers lack reliable and organized market linkages for their produce.

Post-Harvest Losses

High waste due to poor handling systems and inadequate storage infrastructure.

Low Value Addition

Limited participation in value-addition activities and processing opportunities.

Market Information Gap

Farmers lack access to real-time market information and pricing data.

Price Instability

Volatile and unpredictable produce prices impact farmer incomes negatively.

Youth Opportunities

Limited employment and income generation opportunities for young people in agriculture.

Circularity & Sustainability

Our circular approach integrates environmental responsibility with economic growth

1

Reducing Post-Harvest Losses

We utilize non-export grade and surplus avocados for oil processing instead of allowing them to go to waste.

2

Recovering By-products

Avocado seeds generated during oil processing are repurposed into AvoChai and seed powder applications.

3

Expanding Resource Efficiency

We explore additional uses for avocado pomace, peels, and biomass to further reduce waste.

4

Sustainable Production

Improving energy efficiency and processing systems, including solar drying technologies.
