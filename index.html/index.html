From African Farms to Global Wellness

Avoglow was born from a simple yet powerful idea: that the most luxurious beauty solutions can be found in nature, and that beauty should empower communities.

Founded in partnership with Atari Farms, we work directly with over 500 avocado farmers across East Africa, ensuring fair wages and sustainable farming practices. Every bottle of Avoglow oil tells a story of circular innovation—transforming avocado seeds, typically discarded as waste, into our signature AvoChai wellness tea.

Our Mission

To create premium, natural beauty and wellness products while building a circular economy that empowers African farming communities and reduces agricultural waste.

Our Vision

To become Africa's leading sustainable beauty brand, proving that luxury and ethics can coexist beautifully.

The Atari Farms Connection

Our partnership with Atari Farms ensures that every avocado is sourced ethically. We provide farmers with training in sustainable agriculture, fair trade certification, and guaranteed purchase agreements. This relationship forms the backbone of our supply chain and our commitment to community development.

Core Values

  • Sustainability: Zero-waste production and circular economy principles
  • Innovation: Transforming agricultural byproducts into premium wellness products
  • Community: Empowering farmers and creating local employment
  • Quality: Cold-pressed, pure ingredients with no additives

The Circular Economy

Nothing goes to waste in the Avoglow ecosystem

1. Farmer Cultivation

Local farmers grow avocados using sustainable agricultural practices, receiving fair trade prices and training in organic farming methods.

2. Cold-Press Extraction

Avocados are cold-pressed within 24 hours of harvest to extract pure, nutrient-rich oil for our beauty products, preserving all essential fatty acids and vitamins.

3. Seed Recovery

Instead of discarding seeds (which constitute 30% of the fruit weight), we collect, clean, and dry them for our wellness line, creating value from what was once waste.

4. AvoChai Creation

Seeds are roasted, ground, and blended with cinnamon, ginger, cloves, and cardamom to create our signature caffeine-free chai, rich in antioxidants.

5. Additional Income

Farmers earn extra revenue from seed sales, increasing their income by 30% while reducing agricultural waste that would otherwise contribute to landfill methane emissions.

Community Development

Beyond fair trade pricing, Avoglow invests in community infrastructure including clean water projects, educational scholarships for farmers' children, and healthcare initiatives. Our circular model ensures that every purchase contributes to a sustainable future for East African farming communities.

Environmental Impact

By utilizing avocado seeds that would otherwise decompose and release methane, we've prevented over 15 tons of CO2 equivalent emissions. Our cold-press extraction uses 40% less energy than traditional refining methods, and all packaging is recyclable or biodegradable.
