From African Farms to Global Wellness

Avoglow was born from a simple yet powerful idea: that the most luxurious beauty solutions can be found in nature, and that beauty should empower communities.

Founded in partnership with Atari Farms, we work directly with over 500 avocado farmers across East Africa, ensuring fair wages and sustainable farming practices. Every bottle of Avoglow oil tells a story of circular innovation—transforming avocado seeds, typically discarded as waste, into our signature AvoChai wellness tea.

Our Mission

To create premium, natural beauty and wellness products while building a circular economy that empowers African farming communities and reduces agricultural waste.

Our Vision

To become Africa's leading sustainable beauty brand, proving that luxury and ethics can coexist beautifully.

The Circular Economy

Nothing goes to waste in the Avoglow ecosystem

1. Farmer Cultivation

Local farmers grow avocados using sustainable agricultural practices, receiving fair trade prices.

2. Cold-Press Extraction

Avocados are cold-pressed to extract pure, nutrient-rich oil for our beauty products.

3. Seed Recovery

Instead of discarding seeds, we collect and dry them for our wellness line.

4. AvoChai Creation

Seeds are roasted, ground, and blended with spices to create our signature caffeine-free chai.

5. Additional Income

Farmers earn extra revenue from seed sales, increasing their income by 30%.

Community Development

Beyond fair trade pricing, Avoglow invests in community infrastructure including clean water projects, educational scholarships for farmers' children, and healthcare initiatives. Our circular model ensures that every purchase contributes to a sustainable future for East African farming communities.
